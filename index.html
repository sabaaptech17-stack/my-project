I’m Umm-e-Saba, a student with a growing interest in technology and creativity. I enjoy learning how websites work, experimenting with layouts and colours, and turning a simple idea into something people can explore.

I’m especially interested in front-end development and visual design. I like my work to feel thoughtful, organised, and a little different — because even small details can make a big difference.

Outside of coding, I enjoy imagining stories, developing creative concepts, and finding inspiration in the little things around me. I’m still learning, still experimenting, and excited to see where each new skill takes me.
